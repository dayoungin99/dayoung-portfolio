The Vanishing Arctic

Built an interactive scrollytelling data visualization exploring Arctic sea ice decline, climate change, and its impact on polar bear habitats.

Live Site GitHub

About Project

The Vanishing Arctic is an interactive scrollytelling experience that visualizes Arctic sea ice decline and its impact on polar bear habitats. Using climate datasets and D3.js, the project transforms complex environmental data into an engaging narrative experience.

Key Contributions

  • Transformed climate and sea ice datasets into interactive visual narratives using D3.js.
  • Designed a scrollytelling experience that guided users through Arctic environmental changes over time.
  • Developed responsive visualizations to improve accessibility and engagement with scientific data.
  • Communicated the impact of climate change on polar bear habitats through data-driven storytelling.
